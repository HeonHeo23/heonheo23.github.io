# 8. Redemption
Resurrection that followed, where God's action most directly addresses our HUM problem.
The HUM death and resurrection of the Son undo our spiritual death and make us alive again.
## 8.1 Christ's Death and The Atonement
Redemption = "buying back"; Atonement = the work of Christ brings people back to God.
1. Classic views, early church and EO today, focus on Christ's life, death and resurrection as God's action to overcome the powers of sin, death and the devil that enslave fallen people
2. Substituionary views, Anselm 11th c. + Western, focus on Christ's death as a payment of the debt we owe to God because of our guilt before them
3. Examplary views focus on the way Christ's life and death motivate us to overcome our sinfulness and live selflessly in imitation of him

No formal declaration by the church about which view was correct. 
The Church affirmed that God the Son
1. came down
2. suffered
3. died
4. was buried
5. rose
6. ascended
